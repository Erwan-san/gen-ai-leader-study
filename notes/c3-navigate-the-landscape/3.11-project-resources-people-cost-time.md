# 3.11 Gen AI project resources: People, cost, and time
Course 3 · activity 651208 · video summarised

## Video: build vs use existing
- True: sometimes gen AI should run **at the edge or locally**, not in the cloud. Every situation differs.
- To decide between using an existing app and building a custom solution, assess **needs and resources**:
  - **Scale:** individual, team, company, or millions of customers?
  - **Customisation:** what makes it unique? Will **grounding or fine-tuning** do, or is a **new model** needed?
  - **Interaction:** how, where and when users interact, which affects **latency and connectivity**.
  - **Resources:** **time, people, budget**.

## People: roles across the stack
| Role | Typical interaction |
|---|---|
| **Business leaders** (business users) | Use **pre-built** gen AI solutions to improve operations and customer experience, e.g. **Google Workspace with Gemini** for content creation, data analysis, document summarisation |
| **Developers** | Build and deploy **custom agents** and integrate AI into existing apps.<br>**Tools they use**<br>**AI Applications**: create custom agents, generate code with AI, process data with AI.<br>**Pre-trained APIs**: integrate AI into apps quickly.<br>**Agent Platform**: build and deploy agents with tools for **orchestration, grounding and action**. |
| **AI practitioners** | **Customise, deploy and optimise** gen AI models with Agent Platform tools. Scale workloads, **integrate models with BigQuery**, implement responsible-AI measures such as **bias detection** and **adversarial testing** |

Ensure you have the **right expertise** before building.

## Cost
### Three things you pay for
1. **Training** the model.
2. **Deploying** the model to an **endpoint**.
3. **Using** the model for predictions.

Training and deploying usually cost **compute time** plus **storage** (training data, outputs).

### Pricing models for using models
| Model | Description |
|---|---|
| **Usage-based** | Pay for what you use, often by **tokens or characters** (common for APIs such as Google's PaLM & Gemini APIs) |
| **Subscription-based** | Recurring fee, often tiered by usage limits or features |
| **Licensing fees** | One-time or recurring, especially for commercial use or embedding in products |
| **Free tiers** | Limited free use for experimentation or non-commercial purposes |

### Usage pricing metrics
- **Tokens:** a token is a piece of text, a word or part of a word.
- **Characters:** some providers, **like Google**, charge per character processed.
- **Requests:** you may be charged per request, regardless of the task's complexity or volume.
- **Compute time:** the time taken to process your requests can add to the cost, especially for resource-intensive tasks.

### Factors that drive cost
- **Model size and complexity:** bigger, more capable models cost more.
- **Context window:** a larger context window (the amount of text the model can consider) can increase costs.
- **Features:** fine-tuning and embeddings may be **priced separately**.
- **Deployment:** where you deploy affects compute costs.

## Time
- More custom = more time and resources:
  - **Pre-built app:** **seconds**.
  - **Custom agent with AI Applications:** **much less time** than fully custom.
  - **Fully custom solution with a custom model:** **months**.
- Check that the timeline is realistic against the requirements.

## Key takeaway
Allocate the **right mix of business users, developers and AI practitioners**, understand **cost** drivers, and match **timelines** to complexity.
