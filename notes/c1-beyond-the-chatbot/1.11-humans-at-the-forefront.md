# 1.11 Keeping humans at the forefront of gen AI implementation
Course 1 · activity 638561 · no video

## Principle
Shift human responsibility **from task execution to strategic thinking**.

## Augmentation: gen AI enhances human strategic work
| Area | What gen AI does | What humans still do |
|---|---|---|
| **Critical thinking & problem solving** | Provides data and insights | Interpret them and make decisions |
| **Creativity & innovation** | Generates ideas, explores options | Push boundaries; true innovation |
| **Relationship building & collaboration** | Eases communication and information sharing | Build trust, foster collaboration, handle human dynamics |
| **Strategic planning & vision** | Forecasting, trend analysis | Set long-term vision and goals, chart the course |

## Automation: gen AI takes over tasks that are
- **Repetitive and rule-based:** data entry, information retrieval, content formatting, basic code generation.
- **Time-consuming and resource-intensive:** research, data analysis, summarisation, first drafts.

## Humans-in-the-loop (HITL) are still needed, even for automation
1. **Data selection and preparation:** high-quality, relevant, representative training data.
2. **Prompt design and refinement:** prompts that get accurate, useful answers.
3. **Output evaluation and refinement:** review and edit for accuracy, relevance and brand alignment.
4. **Continuous monitoring and feedback:** track performance, find improvements.

## Knowledge check: augmentation or automation?
| Scenario | Answer | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A chatbot answers basic pricing and availability questions for sales | **Automation** | Repetitive FAQ work; frees sales for complex deals |
| A financial analyst uses gen AI to analyse data and draft trend reports, then advises clients | **Augmentation** | AI output informs the human's strategic advice |
| A retailer generates catalogue product descriptions from attributes | **Automation** | Labour-intensive content at scale; resources move to marketing and service |
| Developers generate standard code, then review and integrate it | **Augmentation** (via automated code generation) | Boosts developer capability; human oversight ensures quality |

## Key takeaway
Best use = **augment** strategic thinking and creativity, **automate** repetitive tasks. Humans stay central in both (prompt design, output evaluation, monitoring).
