## Gems = personalised AI assistants inside Gemini
Three benefits:
1. **Personalised responses:** tailored instructions and information make answers more relevant to that use case.
2. **Streamlined workflows:** templates, prompts or guided interactions for repetitive tasks.
3. **Reset context:**
   - Set the Gem's context once (background, instructions).
   - Then start **multiple separate chats** with it. Each keeps its own focus and **nothing spills over** between chats.
   - Like different conversations with the same expert.

## Building Gems
- Use **Google's pre-made Gems** for common tasks, or **create a custom Gem**.
- A custom Gem's instructions **prompt the agent on how to act**: "prompting to build your agents".
- **Role prompting** fits well here (e.g. "act as an air traffic controller").
- **Attach documents and resources** for a focused knowledge base, e.g. a "code reviewer" Gem with the project's style guide and best practices, so feedback is **grounded** in your requirements.

## Best practices
- Be **clear and specific**. Provide **examples** of the desired outcome.
- **Refine instructions over time** as you use the Gem.
- Think about **how users will interact** with it and what information they'll need to provide.

## Key takeaway
Gems turn well-crafted prompts into **reusable assistants** using role prompting and a focused knowledge base.
