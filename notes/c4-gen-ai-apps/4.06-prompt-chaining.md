# 4.6 Prompt chaining
Course 4 · activity 646197 · no video

## Definition
- **Prompt chaining:** each response **builds on the previous one**, like a conversation, to reach more complex and nuanced results.
- In a chatbot like Gemini, **earlier prompts affect later ones** (shared context).
- Method:
  1. Start with a **broad initial prompt**.
  2. Follow up with clarifying questions, more specifics, or a **different format**.
  3. Refine **incrementally** until the goal is met, rather than doing everything in one request.

## Exercise: "The chain reaction"
1. **Set a goal:** a business proposal, a business-trip itinerary, a multi-element marketing image.
2. **Start the chain:** e.g. a 5-day Tokyo business trip in August to meet clients and attend **Google Cloud Next Tokyo**, plus landmarks if there's time.
3. **Build the chain:** follow-ups such as hotels near the conference centre with business amenities, or adding day trips.
4. **Observe and analyse** how each step builds on the last.
5. **Reflect and share.**
