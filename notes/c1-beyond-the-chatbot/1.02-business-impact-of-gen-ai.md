# 1.2 The business impact of generative AI
Course 1 · activity 638550 · contains a video (summarised)

## Core idea (video)
- **Generative AI** = a type of AI that creates new content and ideas: text, images, music, code.
- Gen AI is a **technology that can be integrated into applications, not an application itself**. A chatbot is just one application of it.
- **Gemini (model) vs the Gemini app:**
  - Gemini is one of Google's gen AI **models**.
  - The **Gemini app** is Google's gen AI chatbot (help with writing, planning, learning…). It is one application built on the Gemini model.
- Other products built on the Gemini model:
  - **Google Workspace with Gemini**: Gmail, Google Meet, Google Slides, etc.
  - **Gemini for Looker**: analytics.
  - **Gemini in BigQuery**: generate, explain and troubleshoot SQL queries and Python code.
  - Gemini with Google Cloud services: automate creating and modifying resources, troubleshoot issues, suggest solutions.
- Headlines about "the AI revolution" may mean a chatbot app or the broader embeddable technology.

## Multimodal gen AI
- **Multimodal gen AI** means a model can process and integrate several formats (plain text, images, video, audio, PDFs), either individually or combined at the same time.
- Course examples:
  - **Text + image:** marketing creates on-brand social posts with images, captions, hashtags and optimal posting times.
  - **Text + PDF:** a legal team summarises a contract, answers clause questions, flags risks and suggests alternative wording.
  - **Text + video + data:** market research analyses sentiment in video testimonials together with the spoken words and survey data, to find themes, pain points and satisfaction.
  - **Sensor data + video:** manufacturing safety. Camera feeds and sensors detect spills or equipment malfunctions and alert workers.

## Key takeaway
Businesses already use Google Cloud gen AI to support **decision-making**, **efficiency and productivity**, **creative idea generation**, and **cost reduction through automation**.
