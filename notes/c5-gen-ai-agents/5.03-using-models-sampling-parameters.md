# 5.3 Using models (sampling parameters)
Course 5 · activity 646164 · includes a short explainer video (summarised)

## Context
- Models are the **brains** of agents. Even after training and tuning, you can improve results by adjusting **parameters and settings**.
- **Sampling parameters** are the "knobs and dials" you send with your prompt to shape output: more creative, more concise, a given tone.

## The parameters (exam core)
| Parameter | What it does | Rule of thumb |
|---|---|---|
| **Token count** | **Tokens** are the model's text building blocks (word, part of a word, space, punctuation). Models have **token limits**; more tokens = longer conversations but more processing | **1 token ≈ 4 English characters**; **100 tokens ≈ 60–80 words** |
| **Temperature** | Controls **randomness / "creativity"** of word choice | **Low** = focused, deterministic, repeatable (good for Q&A and summaries). **High** = random, diverse, unexpected (creative) |
| **Top-p (nucleus sampling)** | Samples from the **smallest set of most-likely tokens whose cumulative probability ≥ p** | **Lower** = more focused. **Higher** = more diversity (includes lower-probability tokens) |
| **Top-k** (video) | Samples randomly from the **top k most-likely** tokens | Gives other high-scoring words a chance. Can behave oddly when the distribution is **highly skewed**, which motivates top-p |
| **Safety settings** | **Filter harmful or inappropriate** content; adjustable filtering level | |
| **Output length** | **Maximum length** of the generated text (words or characters), or let it stop naturally | |

**Recipes:**
- Concise, factual → **lower temperature + shorter output length**.
- Creative, open-ended → **raise temperature and top-p**.

## Explainer video in my words
- The model outputs a **probability distribution** over possible next words.
- Always picking the top word → dull, repetitive text. Sampling fully at random → odd, unlikely words.
- **Temperature** narrows (low) or widens (high) the range of candidates.
  - The video's example: low temperature favours "flowers" (typical); high temperature can pick "bugs" (unusual).
- **Top-k:** e.g. k = 2 picks randomly from the 2 most likely words ("flowers", "trees").
- **Top-p:** e.g. p = 0.75 picks from the smallest set whose combined probability reaches 0.75 ("flowers", "trees", "herbs"). The set **grows or shrinks dynamically** with the distribution.

## How to access parameters
- They vary by provider and access route. Often through **APIs (application programming interfaces)**: a way for software systems to **communicate and exchange information**, "like a messenger".
  - Example: a weather app calling a weather service.
- In gen AI, your app sends the **prompt plus parameters** via the API and receives the response.

## Key takeaway
Tune **temperature, token count, top-p** (and top-k, safety, output length) to steer model behaviour.
