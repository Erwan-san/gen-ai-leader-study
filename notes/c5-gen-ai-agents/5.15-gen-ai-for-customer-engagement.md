# 5.15 Gen AI for customer engagement (Gemini Enterprise for CX)
Course 5 · activity 646177 · no video

## Context
- Customers often don't want to search. They want to **contact the company directly**.
- These touchpoints can be pain points but are **critical connections**. Engagement quality can make or break a company.

## Gemini Enterprise for Customer Experience (CX)
- An **agentic solution** bringing **shopping and customer service together in one intelligent interface**.
- Built on **Gemini multimodal models**: complex reasoning to understand **intent** and do **multi-step tasks**.
- **Components:**
  1. **Customer Experience (CX) agents:** customer-facing chatbots, now proactive **"digital concierges"**.
  2. **Agent Assist:** supports **live human** contact-centre agents.
  3. **Customer Experience Insights (CX Insights):** insight across all customer conversations (bot or human).
  4. All built on **Contact Center as a Service (CCaaS)**: an enterprise-grade, **cloud-native** contact-centre foundation.

## CX Agent Studio: three build approaches
| Approach | Description |
|---|---|
| **Deterministic** | **Rule-based** ("if the user selects X, buy product Y"). Every action explicitly defined. **Low-to-medium code**. **Maximum predictability** for sensitive transactions |
| **Generative** | Powered by **Gemini multimodal models**. Understands intent from **text, images, voice**. Natural, human-like flow. Chooses the best path from instructions plus **grounded data**. Mostly **natural-language prompting, little or no code** |
| **Hybrid** | Combines both: **deterministic control for compliance and security** plus **generative flexibility** for nuance |

- Hybrid agents **take actions** (e.g. **process a refund, schedule a delivery**), not just talk.
- Build options: **rapid deployment with natural-language instructions**, or **sophisticated hybrid workflows** with custom rules and enterprise logic.

## Agent Assist (for human agents)
- Why: human agents vary in experience, and onboarding needs lots of training.
- In-the-moment help:
  - **recommended responses**;
  - **suggested knowledge-base content**;
  - **real-time transcription and translation**;
  - **conversation summaries**;
  - **real-time coaching**.

## CX Insights
- Analyses conversational data **across the customer journey** for contact-centre leaders: efficiency, agent performance, customer experience.
- Signals:
  - **agent and caller sentiment**;
  - **entity identification**;
  - **call topics**;
  - auto-flags interactions for review;
  - **automatic scoring with conditional scorecards**.
- **Generative FAQ in Insights:** shows common customer questions and how they're answered, to find **FAQ gaps**, track trends, improve responses.

## CCaaS
- Contact centres need **24/7, multichannel** (phone, text, email…) operation with security and privacy.
- CCaaS:
  - manages infrastructure;
  - **integrates with CRMs**;
  - offers **omnichannel** support (consistent across web, apps, phone, text);
  - handles simultaneous channels, **channel switching**, **multimodal** interactions, and **agent routing**.
- Integrates with CX agents, Agent Assist and CX Insights.
