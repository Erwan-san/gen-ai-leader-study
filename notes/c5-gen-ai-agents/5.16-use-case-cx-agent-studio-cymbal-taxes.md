# 5.16 Use case: Customer Experience Agent Studio (Cymbal Taxes)
Course 5 · activity 646178 · video summarised · Cymbal = fictitious

## Problem
- **Cymbal Taxes**, a fast-growing online tax-prep service, couldn't keep up with the **volume of inquiries in tax season**. It needed **instant, personalised, 24/7** support.

## Solution
- With **Gemini Enterprise for CX → CX Agent Studio**, they built an **Agentic Tax Assistant** for web and app:
  - **natural-language instructions**;
  - plus a **data store tool** (tax laws, FAQs, customer data).
- **Capabilities:**
  - answer questions about specific tax forms;
  - **personalised advice** for the user's situation;
  - help with **filing and payment**.
- **Sample dialogue (summarised):**
  - The assistant checks home-office deduction eligibility by asking clarifying questions (exclusive use, % of home, self-employed vs employee), confirms eligibility, and offers to walk through the claim.
  - It also gives filing deadlines: federal **April 15**; extension to **October 15**. An extension covers filing, not payment.

## Results (memorise)
- **NPS +15 points**.
- **Call volume −20%**.
- **Email inquiries −30%**.
- Staff refocused on complex work (**audits, complaints**). Better engagement and retention.

## Knowledge check
- Primary challenge solved → **keeping up with high inquiry volume in tax season**.

## Key takeaway
CX agents combining **generative playbooks** with the company's **data stores** give accurate, instant answers and measurable business results.
