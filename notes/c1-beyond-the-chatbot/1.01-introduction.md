## Learning path context
- The path prepares you for the **Google Cloud Generative AI Leader** certification.
- It's for all roles and backgrounds. **No technical experience is required.**
- Course 1 has **no prerequisites**. Being signed in to a Google account is recommended so you can use the tools in the activities.
- The courses are best taken in order:
  1. Gen AI: Beyond the Chatbot
  2. Gen AI: Unlock Foundational Concepts
  3. Gen AI: Navigate the Landscape
  4. Gen AI Apps: Transform Your Work
  5. Gen AI Agents: Transform Your Organization

## Why the course exists (video)
- Businesses want to use gen AI but don't know how. The gap is mostly **non-technical**: what gen AI can be used for, how to get **buy-in**, and how to communicate with technical and non-technical teams.
- Path storyline:
  - C1 introduces gen AI, its business capabilities, and how to plan a gen AI strategy.
  - C2 covers foundational terminology (e.g. LLMs, responsible AI).
  - C3 covers the layers of the gen AI landscape, with the opportunities and challenges at each layer.
  - C4 covers gen AI apps for your individual workflow.
  - C5 covers custom gen AI tools to transform the organisation.
- Throughout, the path shows how Google and **Google Cloud** support building, using and adopting gen AI apps.

## Questions this course answers
1. What is gen AI, and what can it be used for?
2. How can gen AI transform businesses?
3. How can Google Cloud support your gen AI journey?
4. What strategies help implement gen AI successfully in a business?
