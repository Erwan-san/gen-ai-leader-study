# 2.7 Deep learning, generative AI, and foundation models
Course 2 · activity 607917 · no video

## The nesting
**AI ⊃ ML ⊃ Deep learning ⊃ (gen AI built on deep learning; foundation models → LLMs, diffusion models)**

- **Machine learning:** a broad field with many techniques, one of which is deep learning.
- **Deep learning (DL):** a powerful subset of ML that uses **artificial neural networks** (with multiple layers) to handle highly complex patterns and make sophisticated predictions.
- **Semi-supervised learning:** neural networks can train on a **small amount of labeled data plus a large amount of unlabeled data**. They learn foundational concepts and **generalise** to new examples.
- **Generative AI** uses deep learning to create new content: text, images, audio and more. Neural-network deep-learning techniques are **the engine** behind generative models.

## Foundation models
- Use **deep learning**. Trained at **massive scale**, often on vast **unlabeled** data.
- They develop a broad understanding of the world and capture intricate patterns, so they handle varied tasks across domains.
- **Analogy:** a student who has read an entire library and can adapt to many tasks.

## Types of foundation model
- **Large language models (LLMs):** designed to **understand and generate human language**. They translate, write creative content, and answer even open-ended or strange questions. The most common type users meet (e.g. chatbots like **Gemini**). They also power many **search engines**.
- **Diffusion models:** generate high-quality **images, audio, even video** by **iteratively refining noise** (random, unstructured data) into structured data.

## Knowledge check: definitions
- **AI:** broad field of computer science building machines that perform tasks needing human intelligence.
- **ML:** subset of AI; machines learn from data **without explicit programming** and improve over time.
- **DL:** specialised subset of ML using **multi-layer artificial neural networks** for complex patterns.

## Key takeaway
**Deep learning** = the core technology. **Foundation models** = powerful architectures built on deep learning. **Generative AI** = applying those models to create new, original content.
