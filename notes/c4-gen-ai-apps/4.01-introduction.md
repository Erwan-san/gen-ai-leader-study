Course 4 · activity 646192 · video summarised
Recommended: complete Courses 1–3 first.

## Video points
- The best way to grasp gen AI is **hands-on with existing apps**, chatbots included. They don't show every capability but they spark ideas.
- While experimenting, ask two questions:
  1. **Is this use case repeatable?** Would I, or my team, use it again?
  2. **Is gen AI falling short because it lacks customisation?** It misunderstands my specific needs, or the output is "a bit off".
- When either happens, **write it down and raise it with the team**. These are the use cases with the biggest organisational impact (they lead to Gems, Gemini Notebook, and custom agents in C5).

## Questions this course answers
1. How can gen AI fit into your **daily workflow**?
2. What techniques **improve prompting**?
3. Which Google gen AI products need **no development expertise**?
4. How can you build **more automated workflows** for your team?
