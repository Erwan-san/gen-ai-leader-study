# 3.5 Gen AI agents: Beyond just models
Course 3 · activity 651200 · video summarised

## What makes an agent more than a model
- Agents **observe, act and achieve goals**. They use models but go well beyond them.
- Two distinguishing elements:
  1. **Reasoning loop:** the agent's "thinking process", a continuous cycle that lets it analyse, plan and adapt to outcomes.
  2. **Tools:** functions that let the agent **interact with its environment**: access and process data, use software apps, even control **physical robots**. Like apps on a phone, they connect the agent to real-world information and services.
- **Tool examples:**
  - access and update company **inventory**;
  - **schedule meetings** by checking calendars;
  - **auto-order supplies** when stock is low;
  - talk to a robot's **sensors and actuators**.

## Agents vs LLMs
- Reasoning + tools take agents **beyond text generation**. They:
  - **solve complex problems**: analyse, gather data with tools, decide with **minimal human intervention**;
  - **manage multi-step tasks** LLMs alone can't: in-depth research, troubleshooting code, automating across multiple systems;
  - produce results **beyond text or media**.

## The reasoning loop (memorise the 4 steps)
1. **Observe:** gather information about the environment and the task.
2. **Interpret:** process it and assess the situation.
3. **Plan:** choose a course of action toward the goal.
4. **Act:** execute.

It **repeats until the goal or a stopping point** is reached. Complexity varies: from simple **rule-based** loops to complex logic with extra algorithms or **probabilistic reasoning**.

## Advanced prompt engineering frameworks used in the loop
- Can include:
  - simple rule-based calculations;
  - complex thought chains;
  - ML algorithms;
  - probabilistic reasoning techniques.
- Named examples: **ReAct** and **chain-of-thought (CoT)** prompting. Both are covered in Course 5.

## Knowledge check: model vs agent (my answer key; the page shows feedback only after you submit)
- **Writing assistant:**
  - Grammatical, stylistically appropriate text → **Model**.
  - Organising and formatting content to guidelines → **Agent**.
- **Education platform:**
  - Evaluating a short answer for accuracy, completeness, clarity → **Model**.
  - Providing customised materials and exercises → **Agent**.
  - Tracking progress and adapting the plan → **Agent**.

Rule of thumb: generating or understanding content = model. Multi-step orchestration, personalisation over time, actions = agent.

## Key takeaway
Agents = **model + reasoning loop + tools**. They solve complex problems and manage workflows far beyond standalone models.
