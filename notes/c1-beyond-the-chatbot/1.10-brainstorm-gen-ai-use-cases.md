# 1.10 Brainstorm gen AI use cases
Course 1 · activity 638560 · contains a video (summarised)

## The creative matrix (recommended method)
- **Purpose:** systematically generate and then prioritise gen AI use cases.
- **Setup:** gather a team and draw **two axes** (they're flexible):
  - **Axis 1, gen AI options:** organised by product, modality or another dimension.
  - **Axis 2, business-specific items:** personas, workflows or strategic goals.
- **Ideation:** team members write **sticky notes** (physical or virtual) with ideas at each intersection.
- **Evaluation:** score each idea for **impact** and **how much work it takes to build** (feasibility).

## Worked example: faux floral business
- **Business priorities:** Enhance Customer Experience · Increase Efficiency · Drive Innovation · Enhance Employee Productivity.
- **Capabilities:**
  - in the video: Agent Search, Agent Conversation, Agent Studio;
  - in the sample image: Google Workspace with Gemini, Vertex AI Conversation, Vertex AI Search, Vertex AI Studio. These are the older Vertex names.
- **Sample sticky notes:**
  - **Drive innovation × Agent Search:** customers upload a photo to find similar arrangements.
  - **Customer experience × Agent Conversation:** a chatbot for instant support on care, arrangement and ordering.
  - **Efficiency × Agent Studio:** **fine-tune** a model on customer data to **predict demand** per product.

## Practice
- Come up with 3–5 of your own sticky notes. You may use the Gemini app to help, but **validate all ideas**.

## Key takeaway
The creative matrix links **gen AI capabilities** to **business priorities** to produce practical, impactful, workflow-specific ideas.
