## Video points (Erica & Brett)
- User-facing apps are exciting but only **scratch the surface** of gen AI.
- You can **build a custom solution** or use **pre-existing tools** (e.g. Google Cloud's).

- The course covers the **gen AI landscape**: the layers gen AI is built on, at a **high level** (not technical detail).
