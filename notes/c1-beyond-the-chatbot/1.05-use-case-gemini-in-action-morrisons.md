# 1.5 Use case: Gemini in action (Morrisons)
Course 1 · activity 638554 · contains a customer video (summarised)

## Facts
- **Morrisons** is one of the UK's largest supermarkets. The video adds about **1,600 convenience shops** and **2,700 farmers and growers**.
- It built **Product Finder**, an **in-app AI tool**. Shoppers type a product name and get its **aisle and precise location in-store** in real time, even if shelves were rearranged that day.
- **Stack:** **BigQuery** (product and location data per store) + **Vertex AI (now Agent Platform)** + **Gemini models**.
- **How it works:** Gemini and Vertex interpret the shopper's free-text query and translate it into Morrisons' **product ID and location ID**, then look up the location in BigQuery.
- **Adoption:** about **50,000 hits a day over Easter 2025**, with usage growing as shoppers come back.
- Speaker: Peter Laflin, Morrisons director of data analytics and AI.

## Lessons the customer drew
- **Motivation:** staff are often asked where products are. Hard-to-find items (the example is tahini, a small jar among similar jars) frustrate shoppers. The goal was an **online-style search experience in the physical store**.
- **Pre-trained model benefit:** you don't have to exhaustively test the model itself. You test how it behaves **in your environment** and trust Google's part.
- **Speed and team size:** without these products it might have taken **months and a larger team**. The platform let a **small team** do far more and focus on user experience.
- **Ways of working changed:** faster proof-of-concept to value, and a different style of collaboration.

## Key takeaway (as stated on the page)
- The page's takeaway calls Gemini an image-generation tool for product imagery. That wording doesn't match the Product Finder story, which is about **query understanding plus data lookup**.
- For the exam, remember the substance: business results, time and resource savings, and customer engagement through Gemini + Agent Platform + BigQuery.

## Exam hooks
- Use cases that pair a **pre-trained model** with your own **enterprise data** (BigQuery) deliver value quickly with small teams.
