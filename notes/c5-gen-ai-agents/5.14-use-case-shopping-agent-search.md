# 5.14 Use case: Enhancing the shopping experience with Agent Search
Course 5 · activity 646176 · video summarised (unnamed clothing retailer)

## Problem
- Customers struggled to find items and **abandoned searches**.
- **Keyword search** missed intent: shoppers use vague words and describe visual traits.
- Online search needed to replicate a **store associate** who understands what you mean without the exact product name.

## Solution: Agent Search for commerce
1. **Semantic understanding:** synonyms, related terms, **misspellings**.
2. **AI-powered image search:** upload a photo (an old favourite, something seen on social media) and get **visually similar** catalogue items.
3. **Retail recommendations:** personalised suggestions learned from purchases, browsing and queries.

## Results (qualitative)
- Easier discovery → **more purchases**.
- **Happier, more loyal** customers.
- More exploration and **time on site**.

## Knowledge check
- Feature that helped customers find products they **couldn't describe in words** → **AI-powered image search**.
