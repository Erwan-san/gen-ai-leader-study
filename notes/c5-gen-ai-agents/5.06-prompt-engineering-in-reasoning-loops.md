# 5.6 Adding prompt engineering to your reasoning loops
Course 5 · activity 646167 · video summarised

## Video points
- What you built in AI Studio is a **very basic agent**: no tools, and a reasoning loop that just passes input to the model and returns output.
- You level it up by adding **reasoning and logic**, usually called **prompt engineering**.
  - It's an **agent layer before the end-user application** where prompting logic lives.
  - It's "engineering" in a **logical/building** sense, not software. **Anyone who can prompt can prompt-engineer.**
- **Example: a module-summary writer agent.**
  - You could prompt Gemini or make a Gem. But for a **scalable product that transforms the organisation**, build the prompting logic **into the agent**.
  - E.g. **role prompting** (a Google course developer) plus clear directions and **few-shot** examples.
  - End users then only supply the course script.
- **Metaprompting:** pros often use **gen AI to generate their prompts** ("prompting to get a prompt").

## The reasoning loop
- The component that governs how the agent **takes in information, reasons internally, and decides its next action**.
- It's **iterative and introspective** and runs until the goal or a stopping point. Complexity varies.

Four aspects:
1. **Iterative process:** a cycle of evaluating progress and choosing the next best action. It includes steps for **action, tool selection, observation**.
2. **Internal reasoning:** the **language model** supplies the reasoning and logic to think through the steps.
3. **Decision making:** choose the next action, **which tools**, and their **inputs**.
4. **Reasoning frameworks:** prompt-engineering frameworks guide reasoning and planning (→ 5.7).
