# 1.12 Use case: AI-assisted captioning on Google Cloud (Warner Bros. Discovery)
Course 1 · activity 638562 · contains a customer video (summarised)

## Facts
- **Warner Bros. Discovery (WBD)** has a large, unique content library, historically its main business driver.
- With **Google Cloud** (and **Accenture**), WBD built **"Caption AI"**, ML-driven, more efficient captioning.
- **Strategic shift:** content data that was used only for distribution and viewing becomes a **data asset for ML**.
- **Workflow (hybrid, human-in-the-loop):**
  1. Caption AI drops a caption file into a folder, already **time-coded and formatted**.
  2. A human **verifies the words and the sync**, then edits.
- **Time per show:** a half-hour show took about **4 hours** to caption. Now it sometimes takes **under 1 hour**.
- **Headline results:** about **50% reduction in overall costs** and about **80% reduction in time** versus fully manual captioning. Quality is also higher for viewers.
- **Platform point:** Vertex AI (now Agent Platform) is **model-agnostic**. Beyond Google's own models, it lets WBD experiment to find the best model for each use case.
- WBD sees **video as the next frontier** for ML.

## Module wrap-up
- Gen AI solutions **always involve humans** somewhere: employees using tools, or customers using AI chatbots. Keep humans in the process to design a good experience and positive interactions.
