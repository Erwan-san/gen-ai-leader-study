# 4.13 Use case: Gemini in action (Cymbal Retail, BigQuery)
Course 4 · activity 646206 · video summarised · Cymbal = fictitious company

## Problem
- Business users lack the SQL or database skills to get at data.
- **Cymbal Retail's** marketing team (demographics, purchasing patterns, campaign performance) depends on the **data analytics team**. That creates a **bottleneck** and delays insights.

## Solution
- **Gemini in BigQuery with function calling**, wrapped in a **user-friendly app**, so marketers query in **natural language**.
- Gemini **translates the question into SQL**, retrieves the data and presents it understandably.
- **Example questions:**
  - top-selling products in the Northeast;
  - demographics for the latest campaign;
  - holiday promotions vs last year.

## Results (the three named benefits)
1. **Increased agility:** direct data access means faster decisions and campaign adjustments.
2. **Improved collaboration:** analysts are freed from routine extraction for **strategic** work.
3. **Enhanced data literacy:** business users understand their data better, which leads to more insight and innovation.

## Knowledge check
- Primary benefit → marketers ask **natural-language questions and get data without writing complex SQL**.
- Distractors map to other products: fleet management → Gemini in Databases; Python/ML → Colab Enterprise; threat containment → Gemini in Security.
