## Three primary ML approaches
| Approach | Data | How it learns | Course example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Supervised** | **Labeled** | Each input is paired with its correct output. The model learns the mapping and predicts outputs for new inputs. | **House price prediction** from size, bedrooms and location, labelled with sale price |
| **Unsupervised** | **Unlabeled** | Finds natural groupings or structure on its own: **exploratory analysis**, insights you didn't know to look for | **Customer segmentation** from purchase history |
| **Reinforcement** | Interaction + feedback | **Trial and error**: maximise rewards, minimise penalties in an environment | A robot learning a maze (walls = negative, shortcuts = positive); training a pet; self-driving cars; robot optimisation in manufacturing |

- **Labeled data** has tags (name, type, number), applied manually or automatically, that give the data meaning. Examples: cat/dog image labels; reviews labelled positive, negative or neutral.
- **Unlabeled data** is raw and untagged. Examples: unorganised photos, audio streams, uncategorised web traffic logs.
- Reinforcement learning suits cases where **explicit instructions or labeled data can't be provided**.

## Google Cloud examples (know the mapping)
| Use case | Product | Approach |
|---|---|---|
| **Predictive maintenance** from machine sensor data (temperature, pressure, vibration) to predict failure and reduce downtime | **Agent Platform** | Supervised |
| **Anomaly detection**: flag unusual transactions (amount, location, time) to prevent fraud | **BigQuery ML** | Unsupervised |
| **Product recommendations** from browsing and purchase behaviour, continuously refined to maximise engagement and sales | **Agent Platform** | Reinforcement |

## Knowledge check answers
- Game AI learning through rewards and penalties to maximise cumulative reward → **Reinforcement**.
- Spam filter trained on emails labelled spam / not spam → **Supervised**. Labeled data is the defining feature.
- Discovering topics in news articles (**topic modelling**) → **Unsupervised**.

## Key takeaway
The choice of approach depends on the **task and the nature of the available data**.
