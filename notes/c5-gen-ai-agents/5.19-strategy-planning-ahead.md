# 5.19 Strategy: Planning ahead
Course 5 · activity 646181 · video summarised

## Video framing
- Nobody is truly a gen AI "expert" because the field keeps evolving. Some of what you learn will date quickly.
- The skills are a **foundation**. Analogy: **learning to surf**. You learned to balance, but the waves keep changing.
- Have a plan, but stay **flexible**.

## Six steps to plan gen AI integration (exam core)
1. **Establish a clear vision:**
   - define how gen AI fits **corporate strategy**;
   - secure **leadership buy-in**;
   - appoint a **champion** to drive adoption.
2. **Prioritise high-impact use cases:** aligned to business goals. Weigh **technical feasibility, data readiness, transformation potential**.
3. **Invest in capabilities:**
   - **upskilling, recruitment, training**;
   - a culture of **learning and experimentation**.
4. **Drive organisational change:**
   - proactively manage shifts in **workflows, roles, mindsets**;
   - **agile collaboration** and knowledge sharing to scale best practices.
5. **Measure and demonstrate value:** track and assess impact; use data to improve and prove value.
6. **Champion responsible AI:** a robust framework for **fairness, transparency, privacy, security**.

## Plan for impact: how to measure
1. **Define key metrics:**
   - aligned to objectives: **ROI**, increased revenue, reduced costs, efficiency, customer experience, security;
   - **ROI** = financial benefits vs costs;
   - revenue = direct impact on sales and profit.
2. **Collect and analyse data:**
   - tracking, surveys, existing data;
   - e.g. **customer satisfaction before vs after** a gen AI chatbot;
   - look for patterns.
3. **Iterate and improve:**
   - if ROI is lacking, find the practical pain points;
   - adjust: **fine-tune models, update training data, improve prompting, integrate better into workflows**.

## Plan for change
Technology moves fast, and customers and employees expect you to keep up:
- **Regularly review and refine strategy.**
- **Stay informed:** industry news, research, expert opinion.
- **Engage the gen AI community:** conferences, workshops, forums.
- **Invest in training and development** (upskilling).
- **Attract and retain top talent** with a creative, growth-oriented environment.
