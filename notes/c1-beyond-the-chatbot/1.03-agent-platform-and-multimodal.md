# 1.3 Agent Platform and multimodal gen AI
Course 1 · activity 638551 · contains a use-case video (summarised)

## Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform ("Agent Platform")
- **Formerly Vertex AI.** It is Google Cloud's **unified machine learning (ML) platform**.
- Use it to **build, train and deploy** ML models and AI applications.
- It gives access to Google's large gen AI models for multiple modalities (text, code, images, speech) through **Model Garden**.
- You can **tune** Google's LLMs to your needs, then deploy them in AI-powered apps.
- One-liner: Agent Platform is the key to **creating and deploying custom gen AI solutions** for your business.

## Use case: automotive virtual assistant (video)
- **Why multimodal:** business workflows mix many data formats and interaction types. Multimodal gen AI processes images, audio and video alongside text and numbers, which gives a fuller understanding of a process.
- **Problem:** a dashboard engine warning light comes on. Today the owner finds and reads the manual, may still not understand it, goes to a mechanic, and the mechanic has to explain (maybe with images).
- **Solution:** an auto company built a customer virtual assistant using:
  - **Gemini on Google Cloud** to understand what drivers ask.
  - **Agent Search** (on Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform) to find the most relevant answer.
- **Agent Search** is a Google Cloud service that lets developers add advanced search to their apps. It is fully managed and scalable, and handles large volumes of **multimodal** data and complex queries.
- **Knowledge sources indexed:** owner's manuals, customer FAQs, help-centre articles, YouTube videos, step-by-step guides.
- **Customer experience:**
  - The driver describes the problem naturally and can show the warning light with the phone camera.
  - **Intelligent knowledge retrieval** comes from Agent Search.
  - **Interactive problem solving** comes from Gemini: a dynamic dialogue with clarifying questions and step-by-step troubleshooting.
  - Use cases range from engine issues to Bluetooth phone pairing.
- **Results:**
  1. Reduced support costs (fewer calls to service centres).
  2. Improved efficiency (customers find answers fast).
  3. Enhanced accessibility (support regardless of technical expertise).
- **Reflection prompt:** most workflows aren't single-modality end to end. Where could multimodal gen AI augment one of yours?

## Key takeaway
Agent Platform is Google Cloud's **core platform for building and deploying custom gen AI**. It gives access to models like **Gemini** and tools like **Agent Search** for multimodal business solutions.

## Exam hooks
- Agent Platform = formerly **Vertex AI**. **Model Garden** = model access.
- Agent Search = managed enterprise search over multimodal data. Gemini = understanding and dialogue.
- Three benefits: lower support cost, efficiency, accessibility.
