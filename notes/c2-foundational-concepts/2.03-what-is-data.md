# 2.3 What is data
Course 2 · activity 607912 · video summarised

## Why data matters
- ML models **predict the future from existing data**, much as people make educated guesses from experience. Humans use intuition. Models use **probability**.
- ML models **analyse their data, identify patterns, then calculate the likelihood of outcomes** for new inputs.
- **Milk analogy (video):**
  - You judge spoilage from the expiry date and a smell test. You're never 100% sure, but past experience makes the prediction fairly accurate.
  - That pattern recognition is what ML does.
  - **Relevant inputs** (date, temperature) matter. **Irrelevant ones** (the song on the radio) don't.
- **Data = information** in many forms: numbers, dates, text descriptions, images, sounds. Which data is relevant depends on the task.

## Data quality: 5 factors (exam favourite)
| Factor | Meaning | Course example |
|---|---|---|
| **Accuracy** | Wrong data → wrong patterns → faulty predictions | A child learning animals from a book with mislabelled pictures |
| **Completeness** | Enough **size**, and representation within the dataset | Forecasting weather from one day of data vs a large sample |
| **Representative** | Inclusive data; otherwise skewed samples and **biased** outcomes | Customer-preference data missing a demographic |
| **Consistency** | Consistent formats and labels; otherwise the model is confused | A puzzle with some pieces numbered and others lettered |
| **Relevance** | Data must fit the task | London traffic data for Kansas crop yields |

## Data accessibility: 3 factors
- **Data accessibility** = needed data is **readily available, usable and high quality**. It enables thorough training and reduces bias. Without it, even sophisticated algorithms are limited.
  1. **Availability:** data may not exist, or may be behind **paywalls** or restricted for **privacy**.
  2. **Cost:** gathering and cleaning high-quality data is expensive, a barrier especially for **smaller organisations**.
  3. **Format:** data must be in a processable format. Conversion can be slow and technically hard.

## Key takeaway
Data is the foundation of AI. Remember the **8 factors**: accuracy, completeness, representativeness, consistency, relevance (quality) plus availability, cost, format (accessibility).
