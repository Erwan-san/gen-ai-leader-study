# 5.13 The power of search agents
Course 5 · activity 646175 · video summarised

## Why search matters (video)
- Search is **the primary way we navigate the digital world**.
- Bad search hurts: customers can't find products, employees waste time in internal docs, customers move to competitors.
- **Good search = relevance and speed**, plus understanding **what we mean, not just what we say**.
- Google's mission ("organise the world's information…") applied to a **business's own information**: "a personal Google Search for your company".
- **Agent Platform Search** combines **information retrieval + natural language processing + LLMs** to understand poorly phrased queries.

## Agent Search on Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform: two families
### Search solutions
- **General search** for your **public website**. Indexes **structured data in BigQuery** and **unstructured documents in Cloud Storage**.
- **Specialised variants:**
  | Variant | Best for |
  |---|---|
  | **Document search** | Large repositories of **unstructured, text-heavy** documents in Cloud Storage (knowledge bases, archives) |
  | **Media search** | **Images, video, audio**: find assets by content, metadata or spoken words |
  | **Healthcare search** | Healthcare data with **regulatory compliance** support (records, research, guidelines) |
  | **Search for commerce** | **Retail catalogue** search: product discovery, attributes, complex product queries |

### Recommendation solutions
- **General recommendations:** similar content across websites, documents and structured content, based on **user behaviour + content attributes**.
- **Media recommendations:** streaming audio and video, digital publishing.
- **Retail recommendations:** personalised products from **browsing history, purchase data, product attributes**.

## How Agent Search works
- Built on **intelligent data connection + grounding + gen AI**.
- It acts **as an agent**: observes the query or context (the environment) and acts by retrieving or recommending, with **data stores as tools**.
- **Key strength:** grounds LLM responses in:
  - **first-party data**;
  - **curated third-party data**;
  - **Google's knowledge graph (Grounding with Google Search)**.
  This minimises hallucinations. **This is a RAG approach.**
- **Extra gen AI features:**
  - **Search summaries:** a document overview, a product comparison, a synthesis of findings.
  - **Answers and follow-ups:** AI-generated answers to natural-language questions, with follow-up questions.
- **Built for enterprise:**
  - **granular access controls**;
  - **advanced analytics** on search trends and behaviour;
  - **scalable infrastructure**;
  - integration through **APIs and SDKs**, for customer-facing or internal search.

## Key takeaway
Agent Search helps customers and employees **find, understand and decide faster**.
