# 4.11 Gemini Notebook in action
Course 4 · activity 646203 · video summarised
Note: the page intro mentions a "retail marketing team", but the video's scenario is a **venture capital firm**.

## Scenario (VC firm investing in early-stage tech)
- **Data:** market research on emerging tech, startup financial statements, economic forecasts, competitor portfolio and funding strategies.
- **Problems:**
  - too much data to sift;
  - risk of missing insights;
  - documents **scattered across departments and platforms** → **information silos**.
- **Solution:** upload everything into a **shared Gemini Notebook workspace** → a **single source of truth**.
- **What they can then do:**
  - **Targeted questions:** e.g. projected growth of the AI-powered healthcare sector; key risk factors for a startup.
  - **Analyse specific aspects:** e.g. summarise startup X's financials with a focus on **user acquisition costs**.
  - **Uncover hidden connections:** e.g. correlation between early adoption and long-term success.
- **Outcome:** they identified a promising startup in **AI-driven medical diagnostics**, got faster decisions and better outcomes, and strengthened their data-driven reputation.

## Benefits of shared notebooks
- Build understanding together.
- Produce **collaborative summaries and reports**.
- Spot **connections and contradictions** across perspectives.
- Stay **up to date**.
- A collective "brain" and a culture of shared learning.
