# 5.5 Parameter playground
Course 5 · activity 646166 · hands-on lab (no video)

## Steps in Google AI Studio
1. **Open Google AI Studio:** log in with a Google account, accept the terms, select **Playground**.
2. **Write a creative prompt:** a pitch to convince investors in a fictional product. Example: an on-campus food-delivery app for students. Press **Run** and review the response.
3. **Experiment with temperature:** in **settings**, move the slider.
   - **Lower** → more **formal, factual** tone.
   - **Higher** → more **passion and enthusiasm**.
   - Rerun the same prompt.
4. **More parameters:** open **Advanced settings**.
   - Change **Output length** and rerun.
   - Change **Top P**: lower → **focused, concise**; higher → **elaborate, varied**.
5. **Observe and analyse:** confident and authoritative? Concise or detailed? Does it evoke the desired emotion?

## Key takeaway
Hands-on tuning of **temperature, top-p and output length** is the best way to learn to control **tone, style and creativity**.
