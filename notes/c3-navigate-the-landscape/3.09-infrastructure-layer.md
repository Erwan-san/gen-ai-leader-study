# 3.9 The infrastructure layer
Course 3 · activity 651205 · no video

## Definition
- The **infrastructure layer** = **hardware + software** providing **compute, storage and networking** to **train, deploy and scale** AI models.
- It's essential for massive datasets and computations, especially LLMs and gen AI. Google has invested heavily in AI infrastructure.

## Scenario motivating it
- A bold gen AI project stalls: in-house servers lack processing power, training takes hours to days, launch is delayed, and scaling your own hardware costs too much.
- → Use **high-performance computing** in the cloud.

## Components
### High-performance computing
- **GPUs and TPUs:** specialised processors that excel at **parallel processing**, crucial for training large neural networks.
  - **GPUs** were originally for **graphics rendering** and are now used for general parallel compute in AI.
  - **TPUs** are **Google's custom chips optimised for AI**.
- **Hypercomputers:** a **supercomputer built by linking many nodes** (containing GPUs and TPUs) over high-speed networks into one massive unit, for the most demanding gen AI models.

### High-performance storage
- Gen AI is **data hungry** (often **petabytes** of text, images, code). Storage must give **capacity and speed**.
- **Large-scale storage systems:**
  - **high throughput** (work done per period of time);
  - **scalability**;
  - dense compute clusters for faster training and **inference**;
  - **high reliability and durability** (data loss would be catastrophic).
- **Fast storage access:** fast read and write so training isn't bottlenecked.

### Networking
- **Google's global fiber network:** **high-bandwidth, low-latency** links that coordinate processors in a cluster.

## Benefits of Google Cloud infrastructure
**Scalability, performance, cost-effectiveness.**
