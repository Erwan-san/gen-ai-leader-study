# 2.1 Introduction to gen AI foundational concepts
Course 2 · Gen AI: Unlock Foundational Concepts · activity 607910 · video summarised
Prerequisite: Course 1 is recommended first.

## Why gen AI is suddenly everywhere (video: Brett & Joe)
- **AI** = creating machines that do tasks that typically need human intelligence. The field is **not new**.
- AI is already inside everyday tools: social media newsfeeds, voice assistants, shopping recommendations, autocomplete.
- **What changed (around 2022):**
  1. **LLMs** became dramatically more capable: they understand complex ideas and produce human-sounding text.
  2. **Computing power** became widely available for training and deploying models: interconnected high-power computers plus hardware and tools built for AI.
  These two together produced today's gen AI explosion.

## Questions this course answers
1. What's the difference between **AI, ML and gen AI**?
2. How do **data types, data requirements and ML approaches** enable gen AI?
3. What **Google Cloud strategies** address **foundation model limitations**?
4. What are the key challenges for **responsible and secure AI** in an organisation?
