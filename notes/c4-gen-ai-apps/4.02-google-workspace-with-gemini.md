# 4.2 Google Workspace with Gemini
Course 4 · activity 646193 · video summarised
Naming: formerly **"Gemini for Google Workspace"**, rebranded **Google Workspace with Gemini** (same features).

## Video anecdotes
- **Gemini in Google Docs:** a phrase sounded too formal. Select it → Gemini icon → "make more casual". That was too casual, so she used **rephrase** instead.
- **Gemini in Google Drive side panel:** asked "what is the definition of Project Horizon?". Gemini returned the definition **and pointed to the source document**. It saved roughly 5 minutes of searching and a colleague's time.
- Two reasons the course covers Workspace:
  1. It shows the **same underlying gen AI technology** looking different in different workflows.
  2. The tools are genuinely useful.

## Key facts
- **Google Workspace** = cloud productivity and collaboration tools: **Gmail, Docs, Sheets, Meet, Slides**.
- Gemini is a **model** used in many apps. Workspace with Gemini puts it **inside Workspace apps**.
- The features available depend on your **Workspace edition**.
- **Business and enterprise licences** add **enterprise-grade data protection**. It extends to Workspace with Gemini features and the Gemini app (with Gemini Advanced), plus **admin features** to manage usage and access.

## Features by app
| Feature | What it does | Course example |
|---|---|---|
| **Gemini side panel** | Summarise, analyse, generate using your emails and docs **without switching apps or tabs** | A new hire asks Drive: "summarize the goals and current status of Project Phoenix" |
| **Gemini in Gmail** | Write and refine emails | HR drafts a company-wide email about a new benefit (eligibility, effective date) |
| **Gemini in Docs** | Create, refine, proofread in the document | Marketing generates taglines and 3 social posts for an athletic shoe campaign |
| **Gemini in Slides** | Generate **photorealistic images** for presentations | Describe subject, setting, style |
| **Gemini in Sheets** | **Generate formulas** from plain language | "find cell C1 in range D:G and output the value in column G" |
| **Gemini in Meet** | **Real-time transcription and translation** for multilingual meetings; **automated summaries and action items** | Global teams |

## Beyond the core apps
- **Google Vids:** online video creation and editing for Workspace users. **"Help me Create"** generates a first draft video. E.g. a PM shares updates, timelines and insights more effectively than an email or report.
- **AppSheet:** a **no-code app builder** included in Workspace **enterprise** editions. **Gemini in AppSheet** builds an app structure (tables, columns, links) from a natural-language description (e.g. "Manage inspections of my facility"). You review and edit it in the AppSheet editor.

## Key takeaway
These features bring **AI-enhanced workflows** into daily work for productivity and effectiveness.
