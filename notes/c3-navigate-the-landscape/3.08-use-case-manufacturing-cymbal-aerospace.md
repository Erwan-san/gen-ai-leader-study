# 3.8 Generative AI in action: A manufacturing use case (Cymbal Aerospace)
Course 3 · activity 651204 · video summarised · **Cymbal is a fictitious company used across Google courses**

## Problem
- **Cymbal Aerospace** makes critical **jet engine components**. It has a huge library of **3D CAD models**.
- Getting information out of them (**material composition, dimensions, stress points, tolerances**) was **manual, slow, error-prone**. That slowed design, analysis and manufacturing.

## Solution: a two-layered approach on Agent Platform
1. **Agent Platform AutoML:** trains a specialised ML model to **extract key metadata from CAD files**, with no deep ML expertise needed. It acts as an "intelligent interpreter" of the files.
2. **Gemini within Agent Platform**, integrated through **MLOps tools such as Agent Platform Pipelines:** turns the raw metadata into **clear natural-language summaries and answers**.
   - Example question: *"What is the tensile strength of the turbine blade?"* → an immediate, accurate answer.

## Platform benefits cited
- **Simplified model development:** AutoML handles training complexity.
- **Customised solutions:** a model trained on their own data.
- **Efficient scaling:** process large volumes of CAD files.
- **Streamlined deployment:** **serverless** infrastructure; Agent Platform handles MLOps (scaling, infrastructure).

## Results (memorise)
- **Product development cycles 15% faster**.
- **Design review time reduced 20%**.
- **Manufacturing planning bottlenecks eliminated** → faster time-to-market and competitive advantage.

## Key takeaway
Combining **AutoML (specialised extraction)** with **Gemini (natural-language understanding and summaries)** on Agent Platform solves industry-specific problems.
