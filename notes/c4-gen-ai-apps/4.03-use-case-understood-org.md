# 4.3 Use case: AI-enhanced workflows in Google Workspace (Understood.org)
Course 4 · activity 646194 · customer video summarised (speaker: Nathan Friedman, CMO)

## Facts
- **Understood.org** is a social-impact organisation supporting people with **learning and thinking differences** (e.g. ADHD, dyslexia), which affect **70M+ Americans**.
- Used **Google Workspace with Gemini** and the **Gemini app** to:
  - **surface trends**;
  - **build donor profiles**;
  - **tailor communications** for fundraising.
- **Workflow:**
  1. The research team (a "power user") asks Gemini trend questions and has it **cite its sources**.
  2. The team **verifies** the outputs and **exports them to a Google Doc**.
  3. They hand off to the partnerships team.
  4. Partnerships uses Gemini to write **personalised messages** to donors and partners, edited and sent with confidence.
- **Results (memorise):**
  - **~10 hours saved per research project**;
  - partnerships team saves **~2 hours per day**.
- **Accessibility and cognitive load:** the CMO (who has learning differences himself) uses Gemini to stay organised and reduce the cognitive load of context switching.
- They also **migrated to Google Meet** to explore Gemini for meeting productivity and accessibility.

## Knowledge check
- Key benefit → **saving time on repetitive tasks and personalising communications**.
- Not: eliminating humans. Not: one-off email writing.

## Key takeaway
Workspace with Gemini is more than content generation. It boosts **organisational efficiency and customisation**. Note that **humans verify** outputs (HITL).
