# 3.13 Choosing and maintaining your gen AI solution
Course 3 · activity 651210 · no video

## Comparing providers and models
1. **Evaluate model capabilities:** use **benchmarks** and comparisons for quality and performance.
2. **Compare pricing structures:** estimate costs from your expected usage and each provider's pricing metrics.
3. **Factor in additional costs:** data storage, API calls, fine-tuning.
4. **Read the fine print:** usage limits, data privacy policies, terms of service.

**Where to compare:**
- provider **pricing pages**;
- **research papers and independent benchmarks**;
- **community forums and discussions**.

## Maintenance: "a journey, not a destination"
- Plan it **from the start**: who maintains it, with what resources, and what the specific needs are.
- **Google Cloud's fully managed environment** lets developers focus on **agent development**, not infrastructure. The platform handles deployment and model improvement, which maximises ROI and avoids costly issues.

| Maintenance area | What to plan |
|---|---|
| **Model monitoring and retraining** | Continuous performance monitoring; periodic retraining with updated data |
| **Data updates** | New sources, cleaning, adapting to changing formats |
| **Software updates and bug fixes** | Keep the platform, libraries and frameworks current for performance and security |
| **Hardware and infrastructure** | Server maintenance, security updates, **capacity planning** |
| **Security and compliance** | Review and update against evolving threats; follow changing privacy regulations |

## Course 3 wrap-up
You covered the gen AI **layers**, Google Cloud options, and the business factors. Leaders must plan to **adapt, update and optimise** over time to keep delivering value.
