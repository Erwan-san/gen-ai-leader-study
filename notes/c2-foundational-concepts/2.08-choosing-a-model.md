# 2.8 Choosing a model

## 8 factors when choosing a gen AI model (exam favourite)
1. **Modality:** the input and output data types (text, image, video, audio).
   - Single-type apps need a model optimised for that modality.
   - Mixed apps (image → caption, video with audio) need a **multimodal** model.
2. **Context window:** how much information the model considers at once.
   - Larger = remembers more of the conversation or document, so outputs are more coherent for long or complex tasks.
   - But larger usually means **higher compute cost**. Balance need against resources.
3. **Security:** encryption, access controls, vulnerability management, compliance with industry standards and regulations. Paramount for sensitive data.
4. **Availability and reliability:** consistent availability under load: **uptime guarantees, redundancy, disaster recovery**. Crucial in production.
5. **Cost:** pricing by usage, compute time, etc. Weigh against budget and expected value.
   - **Match the model to the task. Bigger isn't always better, and multimodal isn't always needed.**
6. **Performance:** accuracy, speed, efficiency. Check benchmarks and relevant datasets. There is a trade-off with cost.
7. **Fine-tuning and customisation:** for specialised use cases, prefer models that can be further trained on your domain dataset.
8. **Ease of integration:** well-documented **APIs and SDKs** to fit existing systems and workflows.

## Knowledge check answers
- **Gemini vs Gemma:** Gemini = multimodal. Gemma = lightweight open models for local or specialised use.
- **Nano Banana vs Veo:** Nano Banana = text-to-image diffusion. Veo = video from text or images.

## Key takeaway
Choose using **modality, context window, security, availability, cost, performance, fine-tuning, integration**. Access and customise Google models through **Agent Platform**.
