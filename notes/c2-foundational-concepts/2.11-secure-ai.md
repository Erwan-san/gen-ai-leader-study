## Video framing
- Build technology responsibly: it should **benefit the world and not cause harm**, and **others shouldn't be able to harm it**.
- Harms to consider: **privacy**, **misinformation**, **fair and ethical treatment** of people.

## Definition
- **Secure AI** = **preventing intentional harm** to your applications: protecting AI systems from **malicious attacks and misuse**.
- Security must cover the **full lifecycle, from development to deployment**: data, infrastructure, and how and where apps are deployed.
- Contrast: Responsible AI (2.12) covers **intentional and unintentional** harm.

## Security across the ML lifecycle
- **Gather your data:** secure data at all times. Use **access controls** on who can access the data and who can **add or input** data.
  - **Data poisoning:** attackers **corrupt training data with manipulated data**, so the model learns wrong patterns and makes flawed (biased, inaccurate, harmful) predictions.
  - Analogy: secretly swapping ingredients in a recipe.
- **Stay informed:** constant vigilance about the latest threats and research.

## Secure AI Framework (SAIF)
- Google's framework for **security standards for building and deploying AI responsibly**. A comprehensive approach to **AI/ML model risk management**.
- Helps organisations:
  - **find and stop threats**;
  - **automatically strengthen defences**;
  - **manage each AI system's unique risks**.
- Integrates with existing company security so models are **secure by default**.

## Google Cloud security tools
- **Secure-by-design infrastructure:** global network and hardware, **encryption in transit and at rest**.
- **Identity and Access Management (IAM):** fine-grained control over access to and usage of resources.
- **Security Command Center:** a **centralised view of security posture** across the Google Cloud environment.
- Workload **monitoring** tools.

## Threat research and expertise
- **Google Threat Intelligence Group (GTIG)** and **Mandiant** share research to identify and disrupt malicious activity.
- **Mandiant Consulting:** specialised guidance and frontline experience to secure AI use and to use **AI as a force multiplier for cyber defence**.
- Goal: move from basic protection to **active validation and operational readiness**.

## Key takeaway
AI brings risks like **data poisoning, model theft and prompt injection**. **SAIF** plus Google Cloud security tools give AI apps a secure foundation.
