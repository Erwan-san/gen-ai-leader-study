## Google as an AI-first company
- **Since 1998**, Google has pioneered AI-driven products (Search, Gmail, Android).
- **Mission:** "organize the world's information and make it universally accessible and useful". Google shares its research, best practices and advances with the world.
- **2008:** Google launched **Google Cloud**, making the same technology available to businesses so they don't start from scratch.
- **Build on Google's foundation:** a full ecosystem lets you use the latest gen AI **without years of investment** building it yourself.

## Five ways to leverage Google's gen AI ecosystem
1. **Individual productivity and efficiency.** Gen AI is built into everyday tools:
   - **Google Search** (Gemini in Search): find information faster and more accurately.
   - **Google Workspace with Gemini**: draft emails in Gmail, generate presentations in Slides, summarise meeting notes in Docs, automate tasks in Sheets.
   - **Gemini App**: personalised assistance and creative exploration.
   - **Gemini for Google Cloud**: build apps and services on Google Cloud with Gemini models.
2. **Continuous improvement.** Google keeps you current:
   - **Automatic model upgrades**: no infrastructure to manage, no retraining.
   - **Access to new features**: early access to new Gemini capabilities.
   - **Security patches and updates**.
3. **Responsible AI.** Google builds securely and ethically and provides education:
   - **Secure AI Framework (SAIF)**: tools and best practices for secure AI systems.
   - **Mandiant**: threat intelligence and expertise against cyberattacks.
   - **AI Principles**: Google's published principles guiding AI development and deployment.
   - **Responsible AI Toolkit**: resources for fair, unbiased, socially beneficial AI.
4. **Enterprise ready.** Covers security, data governance and compliance:
   - **Agent Platform**: unified ML platform (including Gemini) with enterprise-grade security, scalability and compliance.
   - **Google Cloud's security infrastructure**: encryption, access control, network security.
   - **Compliance certifications**: e.g. **ISO 27001, SOC 2, HIPAA**.
5. **Open approach.** No lock-in, so you can choose what fits:
   - Contributions to **TensorFlow and PyTorch**.
   - **Open models and datasets** released to researchers.
   - Support for **open standards** for AI interoperability and data exchange.

## Embracing gen AI
- You don't need to be a technical expert. The principles are easy to apply across functions.
- The most successful implementations have:
  - a **clear vision**,
  - a focus on **impactful use cases**,
  - **tracked results**.
  These create competitive advantage.
