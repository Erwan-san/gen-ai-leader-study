# 5.18 Gemini Enterprise app
Course 5 · activity 646180 · no video

## Why
- A powerful transformation lever is supporting **internal knowledge workers**, not only external customers.
- Employees search across many systems: analytics, productivity, content, **CRM**, communications, portfolio management, supply chain, enterprise planning.
- Goal: make enterprise information **more discoverable**.

## What it is
- AI-built **customised agents** that access and understand data **wherever it's stored**. They can be embedded in internal sites or dashboards: a "personal research assistant for work".
- **The launch point for all your enterprise-ready expert agents.**
- What you can connect or add:
  1. **Gemini Notebook Enterprise:** upload information to analyse, get insights, **audio summaries**.
  2. **Multimodal search agents** grounded in structured and unstructured data across systems (**Google Drive, Confluence, Jira, ServiceNow**, more).
  3. **Gen AI assistants** grounded in enterprise data that **take actions through connectors (tools)**, e.g. create **Google Calendar** events, edit **Jira Cloud** issues.
  4. **Custom agents** built with **CX Agent Studio**.

## Use case: wealth management advisor
### Client trade idea generation
1. Retrieve and compare the latest investment reports for market indicators.
2. Attach **Gemini Notebook "Client Notes"** to personalise.
3. An agent evaluates the research against those notes and finds **insufficient regional and sector diversification**.
4. Upload a spreadsheet, run it through the company's **risk calculator**.
5. Ask Gemini to **draft the client email**.

### Deep market research on a complex political topic
1. **Limit sources** to trusted, curated repositories (including a bank of **government reports**).
2. The agent first **produces a research plan**, which the advisor **verifies** (HITL).
3. The agent executes: searches **thousands of sources**, asks new questions, continues until satisfied.
4. Output: a **detailed report with all source links** plus an **audio summary**.

## Gemini Enterprise app vs Gemini Notebook Enterprise (exam favourite)
| | Gemini Notebook Enterprise | Gemini Enterprise app |
|---|---|---|
| Purpose | **Deep dive into specific documents and web sources** you add | **Comprehensive enterprise AI assistant** |
| Scope | **Only** your uploaded sources | **All connected business systems** via **agents and unified search** |
| Actions | Q&A, summaries, new content from those sources | **Automates tasks** and finds information across systems |

## Key takeaway
- A **central, secure platform for business-specific AI agents** on Google Cloud infrastructure.
- It helps employees find information, understand data and **automate tasks**.
- For teams across **marketing, sales, HR, software, R&D**.

## Exam wording & corrections
- The exam guide lists Gemini Enterprise capabilities as **Gemini Notebook API**, **multimodal search** and **custom agent** capabilities. The Notebook API gives programmatic access to notebooks from your own apps and workflows.
