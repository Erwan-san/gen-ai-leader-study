## Definition
- **Role prompting:** guiding an LLM by assigning a **specific role or persona** (a business analyst, a Shakespearean actor, a helpful customer-service agent…).
- It shapes the **style, tone and focus** of responses.

## Example prompts on the page
- "You are a **customer service agent** for a telecommunications company with **ten years of experience**. A customer is asking about a bill higher than expected."
- "You are a **marketing copywriter** for a new athletic-wear line. Write a running-shoe product description emphasising comfort and performance."

## Exercise
- Template: **"You are a [persona]. Please write a [task]."**
- Bonus: rerun the same task with a different persona and compare.

## Key takeaway
A role makes the model act as an **expert or character**, so content is tailored. Role prompting is also the recommended way to write **Gem instructions** (4.8).
