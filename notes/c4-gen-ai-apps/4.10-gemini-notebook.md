# 4.10 Gemini Notebook
Course 4 · activity 646202 · demo video summarised

## What it is
- An **AI-first notebook grounded in your own documents**, built on **Gemini models**. It works as a **virtual research assistant**.
- You ground it in **documents, presentations, audio and video files**. It **only uses those sources** to answer and summarise.

## How it works
1. **Create a notebook:** a container per project or topic. Title it and upload sources.
2. **Chat interface:** one ongoing chat per notebook. Answers come **only from that notebook's sources**.
3. **Save insights as notes:** keep valuable responses as an organised set of notes.
4. **Share and collaborate:** **shared notebooks** with the same sources, shared questions and shared notes.

## Gemini Notebook vs Gemini and Gems
- **Gems:** general tasks and workflows.
- **Gemini Notebook:** **deep understanding of a specific set of information**.
  - **Hyper-focused knowledge:** only your sources, not a broad knowledge base.
  - **Interactive learning:** questions, different summaries, **quizzes**.
  - **Source-based answers:** traceable with **citations**. If the sources don't cover a question, it **says it can't answer**. It doesn't invent or speculate.

## Use cases
- **Training materials and onboarding:** a shared notebook with the handbook, product docs and training decks for new hires.
- **Researching a new topic:** articles and videos → summaries and Q&A.
- **Audio Overviews:** a **podcast-like** discussion between **two AI hosts**, downloadable.
- **Presentation prep:** generate speaker notes and practice questions from slides.
- **Summarise documentation:** investment and legal teams upload financial statements, legal documents and market research to find key information and **flag risks or inconsistencies**.
- **Project proposals and plans:** a shared notebook keeps everyone aligned.
