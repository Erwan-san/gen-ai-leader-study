# 2.9 Google strategies for foundation model limitations
Course 2 · activity 607919 · video summarised

## Grounding intro (video)
- The video stages a skit: the presenter "hallucinates" absurd claims (talking cats, people flying). Gen AI is creative but can **hallucinate**, losing touch with reality.
- **Grounding** = a reality check. It **ties a model's answers to specific sources**, making output more **accurate and relevant**.
- **Enterprise context:** connect the AI to the **company's own data** (internal documents and reports, customer data, even code). It then learns the company's language and gives **tailored**, not generic, answers.
- Grounding is a **safety net** and essential for **trustworthy** AI. The next lesson covers **fine-tuning and RAG**.

## The 6 foundation model limitations (exam favourite)
| Limitation | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Data dependency** | Performance depends heavily on large training data. Bias or incompleteness in the data flows into outputs (like writing an essay on a book you haven't read). |
| **Knowledge cutoff** | The **last date the model was trained on new information**. It doesn't know later events and can give outdated or incorrect answers; models don't auto-update. Example: a 2022 cutoff means no knowledge of post-2022 events. |
| **Bias** | Training data may contain bias (an **unbalanced dataset**). Statistical learning can **amplify** even subtle biases. |
| **Fairness** | Even with balanced data, **defining fairness is complex** and interpreted in many ways. Fairness assessments cover only **specific categories of bias**, so benchmarks never give the full risk picture. |
| **Hallucinations** | Outputs that aren't accurate or based on real information. Models **can't verify against external sources**, so answers can sound convincing yet be wrong. A major concern in accuracy-critical applications. |
| **Edge cases** | Rare, atypical scenarios expose weaknesses: errors, misinterpretations, unexpected results. |

## Key takeaway
Know all six: **data dependency, knowledge cutoff, bias, fairness, hallucinations, edge cases**. The remedies are in 2.10.
