# 4.4 Prompting techniques
Course 4 · activity 646195 · no video

## Prompting with examples (exam core)
| Technique | Definition | Mnemonic | Online clothing-store example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Zero-shot** | Task with **no examples**; relies on existing knowledge | "Just tell it what you want." | Customer asks about returns; the LLM answers from general knowledge without company-specific training |
| **One-shot** | **One example** to learn from and apply to similar cases | "Guide it with a single example." | One sample inquiry (shipping times); output follows it and tags similar questions |
| **Few-shot** | **Multiple examples** for better task understanding and performance | "Show, don't tell." | Several inquiries with good responses; output follows them (e.g. a discount code for delayed delivery, return instructions for damaged items) |

You can **combine** techniques.
