# 3.3 Agents and gen AI powered applications
Course 3 · activity 651198 · no video

## Definition (memorise)
- A **gen AI agent** is an **application** that tries to **achieve a goal by observing the world and acting upon it** using the **tools** at its disposal.
- Agents **process information, reason over complex concepts, and take actions**. Uses: customer service, employee productivity, creative tasks.
- You can use **pre-built** agents and apps or build **custom** ones.
- **AI Applications** (Google Cloud) lets teams create agents using **natural language** instead of complex code.

## What agents bring to applications
1. **Understanding and responding to natural language:** more intuitive interfaces, e.g. chatbots that grasp complex requests.
2. **Automating complex tasks:** multi-step processes (gather info → decide → act).
3. **Personalisation:** learn user preferences and tailor the experience.

## Role split
- **Applications** = the user-facing layer. They provide **structure and context**: the UI, overall goals, and the specific tasks agents help with.
- **Multi-agent system** = several specialised agents working together in one app.
- Examples:
  - **Travel booking app:** one agent finds flights and hotels; another suggests activities. The app offers browsing and reservations.
  - **Customer support app:** an agent answers FAQs, troubleshoots, and **escalates complex issues to humans**. The app provides the chat UI and integrations.
  - **Personalised learning app:** an agent assesses knowledge, recommends materials, generates exercises. The app structures lessons and tracks progress.

## Key takeaway
**Agents add intelligence and automation. Applications provide the framework and purpose.**
